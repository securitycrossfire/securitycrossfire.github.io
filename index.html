Blue teamer. Threat hunter. Cyber defender.

I am drawn to the part of cybersecurity most people never see: the signals buried in noise, the subtle traces left by an adversary, and the decisions that can change the outcome of an attack.

My world is blue teaming — connecting clues, challenging assumptions, thinking like an attacker, and building defenses that hold up when things get interesting. Some days that means hunting; others, engineering detections, unraveling incidents, or making security operations smarter through automation.

What keeps me hooked is the puzzle. Find the pattern. Explain the unexpected. Stay one move ahead. For me, cybersecurity is equal parts curiosity, engineering and defending what matters.
